Send the same audio to every transcription provider and compare accuracy, turnaround and cost side by side.

!Before you start: connect the providers in MeetStream

MeetStream runs every provider for you, but it needs each one's key. Do this once per MeetStream account.

  1. Open the MeetStream dashboard.
  2. Go to Integrations → Transcription.
  3. Connect Deepgram, AssemblyAI, Sarvam and JigsawStack with your key for each.
  4. Mia Transcribe is MeetStream's own engine and needs nothing.

A provider you haven't connected still appears in the results, marked "not run" with MeetStream's reason. Untick it below if you don't want it.
